Pacific Rhododendron

Name of the Plant

Pacific Rhododendron (Rhododendron macrophyllum)

Common Alternate Names

  • Coast Rhododendron
  • Bigleaf Rhododendron
  • Western Rhododendron

Physical Size

The Pacific Rhododendron typically grows between 6 and 15 feet tall and can spread 6 to 12 feet wide. It is an evergreen shrub with large, leathery leaves and clusters of pink to lavender flowers.

Geographic Range

The Pacific Rhododendron is native to the Pacific Northwest of North America. It can be found from southern British Columbia, through Washington and Oregon, and into northern California.

Preferred Climate

This plant grows best in temperate climates with mild, wet winters and cool to warm summers. It prefers partial shade, moist but well-drained acidic soil, and is commonly found in coastal and forested environments.
