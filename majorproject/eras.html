Album Eras

ATEEZ is known for strong concepts and storytelling that continue across different albums and eras.

Treasure Era

The Treasure series focused on adventure, youth, and chasing dreams. This era introduced the group's pirate-inspired themes.

Fever Era

The Fever era explored youth, uncertainty, growth, and friendship with a more emotional tone.

The World Era

The World era introduced darker concepts, rebellion themes, and more intense performance-focused music.

Golden Hour Era

Golden Hour explores different sides of ATEEZ while continuing the group's strong visual storytelling and performance style.
