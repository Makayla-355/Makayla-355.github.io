Hongjoong

Hongjoong is the leader of ATEEZ and is known for his creativity, stage presence, and involvement in music production. Hongjoong is referred to as Captain.Birthday: November 7th, 1998

Seonghwa

Seonghwa is known for his strong performances and caring personality. Seonghwa's nickname is Mother.Birthday: April 3rd, ,1998

Yunho

Yunho is one of the main dancers and is known for his bright energy.Yunho is referred to as the puppy of ATEEZ.Birthday: March 23rd, 1999

Yeosang

Yeosang is known for his visuals and calm personality.Yeosang like to be referred to as a Doberman but most think of him as a Maltese.Birthday: June 15th, 1999

San

San is recognized for his intense performances and expressive stage presence.San's nickname is Mountain or Grand Duke of the North.Birthday: July 10th 1999

Mingi

Mingi is one of the rappers in ATEEZ and is known for his deep voice.Mingi's reffered to as the Princess of ATEEZ.Birthday:August 9th, 1999

Wooyoung

Wooyoung is known for his playful personality and strong performance skills. Wooyoung is described as the black cat of ATEEZ.Birthday: November 26th, 1999

Jongho

Jongho is the main vocalist and is known for his powerful vocals and the maknae aka youngest memmber.Jongho is also known as baby bear.Birthday: October 12th ,2000
