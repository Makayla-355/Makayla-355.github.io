Member Guide

ATEEZ has eight members. Use this guide to learn more about each member and their role in the group.

Hongjoong Seonghwa Yunho Yeosang San Mingi Wooyoung Jongho

Hongjoong

Hongjoong is the leader of ATEEZ and is known for his creativity, stage presence, and involvement in music production. Hongjoong is often referred to as the Captain.

Birthday: November 7, 1998

Seonghwa

Seonghwa is known for his strong performances and caring personality. Seonghwa’s nickname among fans is Mother because of how caring he is.

Birthday: April 3, 1998

Yunho

Yunho is one of the main dancers and is known for his bright energy. Yunho is often referred to as the puppy of ATEEZ.

Birthday: March 23, 1999

Yeosang

Yeosang is known for his visuals and calm personality. Yeosang likes to be referred to as a Doberman, although many fans compare him to a Maltese.

Birthday: June 15, 1999

San

San is recognized for his intense performances and expressive stage presence. His nicknames include Mountain and Grand Duke of the North.

Birthday: July 10, 1999

Mingi

Mingi is one of the rappers in ATEEZ and is known for his deep voice. Mingi is sometimes referred to by fans as the Princess of ATEEZ.

Birthday: August 9, 1999

Wooyoung

Wooyoung is known for his playful personality and strong performance skills. Many fans describe Wooyoung as the black cat of ATEEZ.

Birthday: November 26, 1999

Jongho

Jongho is the main vocalist of ATEEZ and is known for his powerful vocals. He is also the maknae, or youngest member, and is sometimes called Baby Bear.

Birthday: October 12, 2000
