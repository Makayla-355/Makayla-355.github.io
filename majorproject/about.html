About ATEEZ

ATEEZ is an eight-member K-pop group formed by KQ Entertainment. The group debuted in 2018 and quickly became known for powerful performances, storytelling concepts, and energetic stage presence.

The members are Hongjoong, Seonghwa, Yunho, Yeosang, San, Mingi, Wooyoung, and Jongho.

Performance

ATEEZ is especially known for strong live performances and choreography.

Music

The group explores many styles of music, including EDM, hip-hop, and emotional ballads.

Community

Fans of ATEEZ are called ATINY, combining the words "ATEEZ" and "destiny."

Storytelling

ATEEZ is known for detailed album concepts and connected storylines that continue across different eras and music videos.
