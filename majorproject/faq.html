What does “bias” mean?

In K-pop fandoms, a bias is your favorite member in a group. Many fans also have a “bias wrecker,” meaning another member who constantly catches their attention.

Do I need to know all the lore?

No. ATEEZ has storylines and lore connected to albums and music videos, but many fans still enjoy the music and performances without following every detail.

What are photocards?

Photocards are collectible member pictures included in albums. Collecting and trading photocards is a popular part of K-pop fandom culture.

What content should I start with first?

A good starting point is music videos, performance stages, and short variety content to learn the members more naturally.

Do I need to buy albums to be a fan?

No. Many fans support ATEEZ by streaming music, watching content, or simply enjoying the group casually.

Why does ATEEZ have so many album versions?

K-pop albums often have multiple versions with different concepts, photobooks, inclusions, and photocards for collectors.
