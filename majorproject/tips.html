Fan Tips for New ATINY

Getting into a new fandom can feel overwhelming at first. These tips can help make learning about ATEEZ easier.

Start Slowly

You do not need to watch every video or know every song immediately. Take your time exploring content.

Learn the Members

The Member Guide page is a good place to start learning names, roles, and personalities.

Watch Performances

ATEEZ is especially known for live performances and stage presence, so performance videos are a great starting point.

Have Fun

There is no correct way to be a fan. Everyone explores music and fandom differently.
