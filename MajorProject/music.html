Music Starter Guide

ATEEZ has a lot of music, so this page is meant to give new fans a few easy starting points. These songs show different sides of the group, from powerful performance songs to emotional tracks.

Start with These Songs

  • Wonderland - a powerful song that shows ATEEZ's performance style.
  • Say My Name - a strong beginner song with one of their most recognizable sounds.
  • Answer - a dramatic and emotional song that many fans love.
  • Inception - a good choice for listeners who like emotional vocals and storytelling.
  • Guerrilla - a louder and more intense song that shows their energy.

For Performance Energy

If you want to understand why ATEEZ is known for strong performances, start with songs like Wonderland, Hala Hala, and Guerrilla.

For Emotional Songs

If you want something more emotional, try Answer, Inception, or Turbulence.

For New Fans

My suggestion is to start with a few title tracks first, then move into albums once you know what type of sound you like most.
