Chicken Caesar Smashed Tacos

♡ ♡ ♡ ♡

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground chicken
  • 6 (6-inch) flour tortillas
  • 2 teaspoons Greek seasoning
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 package Caesar salad mix
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Prep Time: 10 mins

Cook Time: 10 mins

Total Time: 20 mins

Servings: 6

Instructions

  1. Spread ground chicken evenly in a very thin layer over each tortilla, spreading the meat all the way to the edges.
  2. Sprinkle each tortilla with Greek seasoning, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Chop the lettuce from the Caesar salad kit into smaller pieces if needed.
  4. Toss the lettuce with the included dressing and cheese packets.
  5. Slightly crush the croutons and set them aside.
  6. Heat a griddle or large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. Spread half of the olive oil over the hot cooking surface.
  8. Working in batches, place the tortillas meat-side down onto the skillet and cook undisturbed until the chicken is browned and cooked through, about 3 minutes.
  9. Flip the tortillas and cook 1 minute more.
  10. Repeat with the remaining tacos.
  11. Top each taco evenly with Caesar salad and crushed croutons.
  12. Fold in half and serve immediately.
