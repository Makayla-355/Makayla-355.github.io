Strawberry Oatmeal Cookies

♡ ♡ ♡ ♡

Ingredients

Cookie Dough

  • 2 cups old fashioned rolled oats
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp. baking powder
  • 1 tsp. baking soda
  • 1 tsp. salt
  • 1 1/2 cups freeze dried strawberries
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2/3 cup brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 2 tsp. vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and line baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Pulse the oats in a food processor about 10 times so they still have texture.
  3. Blitz the freeze dried strawberries until they become a fine powder.
  4. Whisk the oats, 3 tablespoons of strawberry powder, flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together.
  5. Whisk both sugars into the cooled melted butter until smooth.
  6. Add the egg, egg yolk, and vanilla extract and whisk together.
  7. Stir the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until just combined.
  8. Scoop dough into balls and place on prepared baking sheets.
  9. Bake for 9–11 minutes until soft and chewy.
  10. Let the cookies cool for a few minutes before moving them to a wire rack.
  11. Enjoy!

Prep Time: 15 mins

Cook Time: 10 mins

Total Time: 25 mins

Servings: about 24 cookies
