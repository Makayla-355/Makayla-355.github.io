Strawberry Oatmeal Cookies

Scrumptious oatmealy strawberry goodness. Better than your grandma's cookies.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups old fashioned rolled oats
  • 2 cups flour (spooned and leveled)
  • 1 tsp. baking powder
  • 1 tsp. baking soda
  • 1 tsp. salt
  • 1 1/2 cups freeze dried strawberries
  • 1 cup unsalted butter (melted and cooled for about 10 minutes)
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2/3 cup brown sugar (light or dark, packed)
  • 1 large egg (room temperature)
  • 1 large egg yolk (room temperature)
  • 2 tsp. vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and line a couple baking sheets with parchment paper, then set aside.
  2. Pulse the oats in a food processor about 10 times. They should still have some texture and not be finely ground. Place the oats in a mixing bowl.
  3. Add the freeze dried strawberries to the food processor and blitz until it becomes a fine powder. A few little strawberry pieces are okay.
  4. To the oats, whisk in 3 Tbsp. of the strawberry powder, the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Add both sugars to the cooled butter and whisk until smooth. Break up any brown sugar clumps.
  6. Whisk in the egg, egg yolk, and vanilla extract.
  7. Add the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients and stir until just combined.
  8. Scoop cookies with a 2 Tbsp. cookie scoop, roll into balls, and place on prepared baking sheets. Keep the cookie dough in the fridge between batches.
  9. Bake for 9–11 minutes. Do not overbake or they will be dry instead of soft and chewy.
  10. Let the cookies rest on the baking sheet for a couple minutes, then transfer them to a wire rack to cool.
  11. Enjoy!
